Palms, subtropical gardens and the Black Sea after days in the mountains. Batumi’s seafront boulevard and the Botanical Garden at Green Cape make an easy last day before the flight home.

## What a Batumi stay looks like

Rezo keeps this stop light. Typical elements are:

- A morning coffee on the **boulevard** and a walk through the **old town**.
- The **Botanical Garden at Green Cape**, a gentle half-day.
- Dinner by the sea at sunset.

Adjara, the region around Batumi, is also green and mountainous behind the coast, but most travellers come here to slow down.

## Getting here

Batumi sits at the western end of Georgia. From [Kutaisi](/destinations/kutaisi-imereti) it is about 2½ hours; from Mestia in [Svaneti](/destinations/svaneti) about 260 km, five hours. Distances and times are typical, without stops. Your pace sets the real day.

## Climate and season

The climate is subtropical: warm, often rainy, and busiest in summer. If a beach matters to you, say so when you [design your trip](/design-my-trip); if it does not, a single night as the last stop is a gentle way to end the journey. See the [seasons guide](/guides/best-time-to-visit-georgia) for how the year changes the rest of Georgia.
