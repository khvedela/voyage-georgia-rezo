Over the Gombori pass, vineyards run beneath the Greater Caucasus. Kakheti is the heart of Georgian wine, where grapes are still fermented in buried clay *qvevri*, a method on UNESCO’s list of intangible cultural heritage.

## What a Kakheti stay looks like

The pace here is set by lunch. A typical day with Rezo:

- A morning at the **Tsinandali estate** and its garden.
- A visit to a **family qvevri winery**, followed by a long lunch with the winemaker. Rezo chooses the winery to suit the group.
- An afternoon drive to **Sighnaghi**, with time on the town walls at sunset.

Telavi (about 570 m above sea level) is the regional centre and the natural base for the east of the region; Sighnaghi, on a ridge above the Alazani valley, is where many travellers prefer to sleep.

## Further out

The **David Gareja** monastery complex lies in semi-desert on the edge of the region, and is a longer day suited to travellers who want something quieter than the wine trail.

## Telavi is also the gateway to Tusheti

The 4×4 road to [Tusheti](/destinations/tusheti) starts from the Alazani valley, so Kakheti and Tusheti combine naturally in the eight-day [Wine valleys to high villages](/journeys/wine-valleys-to-high-villages) idea.

## Getting here

Kakheti is about 85 km and two hours from [Tbilisi](/destinations/tbilisi). It also links to the north: the road from [Kazbegi](/destinations/kazbegi) to Telavi is a long day of about five hours, which Rezo breaks with a late start and a proper lunch.

If wine is the reason for your trip, read the [Kakheti wine guide](/guides/kakheti-wine-qvevri-guide) first.
