The road north from Tbilisi climbs the Georgian Military Highway into the High Caucasus, and the town of Stepantsminda sits at the end of it, directly beneath Mount Kazbek. For a first trip to the mountains it is the most direct way in.

## The drive north

The highway is the attraction as much as the destination. Typical stops are:

1. **Mtskheta**, about 30 minutes out, for Jvari Monastery and Svetitskhoveli Cathedral (see [Tbilisi](/destinations/tbilisi)).
2. **Ananuri**, a fortress complex on the reservoir about an hour further.
3. **Gudauri**, the ski area, and viewpoints on the pass before the descent to Stepantsminda.

In total that is about three hours of driving, which Rezo turns into a day of roughly 3 h 15 m behind the wheel with time to stop. Take the day slowly and arrive in the afternoon for an evening walk.

## Gergeti Trinity Church

The church stands on a ridge above the town with Mount Kazbek behind it. It can be reached by 4×4 or on foot, and Rezo decides which on the day depending on weather. Plan the visit for the morning, when the mountain is most often clear.

## A second day: Juta and the Chaukhi massif

A mountain road leads from Stepantsminda to Juta village, about 45 minutes away, with an easy walk beneath the Chaukhi massif. It is quieter than the main sights, which is why a third night in Kazbegi tends to be the one travellers remember. Walks are adapted to the group.

## When to go

Summer is the best time for high mountains and walking. In winter the area is a ski region and the road can be affected by snow, which is why Rezo builds slack into every Kazbegi leg. See the [month-by-month guide](/guides/best-time-to-visit-georgia) and the [mountain roads guide](/guides/georgia-mountain-roads-guide).
