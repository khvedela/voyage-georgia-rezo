Sulphur-bath domes, carved wooden balconies and courtyards that open onto wine bars: Tbilisi is where most journeys begin, and for a practical reason. The main airport and the roads out to every region are here, so the first evening can stay light: a walk, a long dinner, a plan for the morning.

## What to do in Tbilisi

The old city rewards walking. Climb to Narikala fortress above the river (there is a cable car up, and a gentle walk down), then drop into the Abanotubani district, where the sulphur-bath domes sit at the foot of the hill. Lunch can be khachapuri and a first glass of qvevri wine, the clay-vessel method Georgia is known for (see the [Kakheti wine guide](/guides/kakheti-wine-qvevri-guide)).

Evenings suit a traditional restaurant rather than anything ambitious. Rezo builds the first day around your flight time, so arrival is never a schedule to beat.

## Day trip: Mtskheta

About 25 minutes from the city, Mtskheta is the ancient capital and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Jvari Monastery and Svetitskhoveli Cathedral sit above the meeting point of two rivers. It is also the first stop on the Georgian Military Highway north, so many routes visit it on the way to [Kazbegi](/destinations/kazbegi).

## How Tbilisi fits a longer route

Tbilisi sits on the Mtkvari river and is the hub for roads east, west and north:

- **North** to [Kazbegi](/destinations/kazbegi) along the Georgian Military Highway.
- **East** over the Gombori pass to [Kakheti](/destinations/kakheti), about 85 km and roughly two hours to Telavi.
- **West** to [Kutaisi and Imereti](/destinations/kutaisi-imereti), about 230 km and 3½ hours.
