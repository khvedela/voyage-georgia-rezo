Over the Likhi range the landscape softens into Imereti. Kutaisi is the base for Gelati Monastery, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Prometheus Cave and the canyons of Martvili and Okatse, with khachapuri imeruli to finish the day.

## A day around Kutaisi

- **Gelati Monastery**: medieval frescoes and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
- **Motsameta Monastery**: a short drive from town.
- **Prometheus Cave**: an easy visit; Rezo often leaves it as an optional afternoon.
- **Martvili and Okatse canyons**: the natural highlights, adapted to the group’s pace.

Lunch in Kutaisi is a good time for khachapuri imeruli, the round Imeretian version of the cheese bread.

## Where it sits on the map

Kutaisi is about 230 km (3½ hours) from [Tbilisi](/destinations/tbilisi) and the natural middle of any east-to-west trip. From here:

- **North to the mountains**: [Svaneti](/destinations/svaneti), about 225 km and 4½ hours to Mestia via Zugdidi.
- **West to the sea**: [Batumi and Adjara](/destinations/batumi-adjara), about 2½ hours.

Tusheti to Kutaisi is a very long way, so an in-between night in Tbilisi keeps the drive easy. Kutaisi also has an international airport, which some travellers use for arrival or departure.
