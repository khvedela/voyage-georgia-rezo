This is a starting point, not a fixed package. Mountain roads are seasonal, so Rezo plans around conditions.

## The idea

Eight days, two halves. First the cellars of [Kakheti](/destinations/kakheti); then the climb into [Tusheti](/destinations/tusheti), where the road is 4×4-only and the villages are stone and slate. The wine comes first so that the trip builds from a long lunch to a remote valley.

## How the days might flow

Arrive in [Tbilisi](/destinations/tbilisi) and head east over the Gombori pass the next day. Two nights around Sighnaghi and Telavi give you the Tsinandali estate, a family qvevri winery and the town walls at sunset. From Telavi, a 4×4 takes the dirt road over the Abano Pass to Omalo and the villages beyond: about 100 km and four to five hours. Three nights in Tusheti make the journey in and out worth it. The last day is the drive back to Tbilisi.

## Season matters

Snow decides when the Tusheti road opens. If your dates are outside the season, Rezo will tell you and offer alternatives. Read the [mountain roads guide](/guides/georgia-mountain-roads-guide) and the [4×4 adventure page](/adventure-4x4).
