This is a starting point, not a fixed package. Route, length, pace and price are always set for you.

## The idea

Ten days is enough for five bases at a slow pace: two nights in most places so you unpack less and see more. The sequence runs east to west, so the mountains come first and the sea comes last.

## How the days might flow

Spend the first two nights in [Tbilisi](/destinations/tbilisi), including the old city and a trip to Mtskheta. Then drive north along the Georgian Military Highway to [Kazbegi](/destinations/kazbegi) for two nights: Gergeti Trinity Church beneath Mount Kazbek, and an easy second day on a quieter mountain road.

Come back down to [Kakheti](/destinations/kakheti) for two nights of vineyards and a proper lunch with a winemaker. Cross the country to [Kutaisi](/destinations/kutaisi-imereti) for Gelati and the caves and canyons, and finish at the Black Sea in [Batumi](/destinations/batumi-adjara).

## What Rezo adjusts

Everything. Add a week in the mountains, drop the sea, spend a night in [Svaneti](/destinations/svaneti), or ask for a slower day. He also decides the order for the season: see the [seasons guide](/guides/best-time-to-visit-georgia).
