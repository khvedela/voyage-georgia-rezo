This is a starting point, not a fixed package.

## The idea

Six days is enough to understand why people return. It focuses on three things that feel different from one another: the capital, the mountains and wine country, with the driving days kept short.

## How the days might flow

Two nights in [Tbilisi](/destinations/tbilisi), including a day trip to Mtskheta’s ancient churches. Then the Georgian Military Highway north to [Kazbegi](/destinations/kazbegi), with Ananuri on the way and Gergeti Trinity Church beneath Mount Kazbek. The road back down continues east over the Gombori pass to [Kakheti](/destinations/kakheti) for vineyards and a long lunch before a last night in Tbilisi.

## Make it yours

Ask for a slower day, an extra night in the mountains, or the sea instead of the vineyards. [Start from this idea](/design-my-trip) and Rezo will do the rest.
